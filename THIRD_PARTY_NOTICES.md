## Additional research skills

The experiment-log, literature-pipeline, and research-proposal workflow concepts were informed by skills distributed by 十五 (JL Lab) under the MIT License. Other Nature-series community skills were reviewed for functional coverage; Biomed Workbench retains its own module contracts, code, schemas, tests, and documentation.

The reviewed Nature-series sources covered these functional groups:

- `nature-academic-search`, `nature-literature-pipeline`, `nature-citation`, `nature-ref-verifier`, and `nature-downloader`: multi-source discovery, citation identity and context, recurring monitoring, and authorised acquisition records;
- `nature-reader`: full-paper bilingual reading and page-addressable source maps;
- `nature-writing`, `nature-polishing`, `nature-proposal-writer`, and `nature-statistics`: evidence-bound drafting, language revision, proposal reasoning, and statistical reporting;
- `nature-data`: data-availability and repository mapping;
- `nature-figure` and `nature-paper2ppt`: figure planning, asset traceability, real presentation packages, and rendered review;
- `nature-reviewer` and `nature-response`: reviewer assessment, response matrices, and revision lineage;
- `nature-paper-to-patent`: disclosure, support, claim structure, readiness, and figure workflows;
- `nature-experiment-log`: stable experiment identity, raw-material traceability, anomaly handling, and archive planning.
