## Journal standards

- Expanded the journal-targeting reference used for project fit and manuscript compliance checks.
- Updated the bilingual journal list, research scope, publisher information, article requirements, and recorded bibliometric fields.
- Kept journal fit based on audience, remit, article type, and evidence maturity rather than acceptance-probability claims.
- Publisher and journal pages remain the authority for title identity, publishing organization, scope, author instructions, article types, and manuscript rules.

## Validation

Automated checks require unique journal identities, bilingual parity, complete structured profiles, and version-bound manuscript review. Historical versions remain selectable for work that was prepared under an earlier standard.
