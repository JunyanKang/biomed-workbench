# 0.2.10 · 2026-09-03

[English](2026-09-03-0.2.10.md) · [中文](2026-09-03-0.2.10.zh-CN.md) · [Release index](README.md)

This release moves Biomed Workbench from a broad collection of methods and provenance rules toward one results-first research path. Routine interaction now centres on the biological question, principal observation, evidence boundary, current progress, and next decision. Environments, parameters, file identity, and complete provenance remain available without occupying the main result view.

## Scientific Questions And Method Selection

- Complex requests are resolved into assay, measurement target, control, normalisation, and biological relation before independent analysis branches and an integration decision are built.
- Negation and terminology disambiguation prevent a “secondary transcriptional effect” from being interpreted as RNA secondary structure.
- Minimal-sufficient analysis favours one primary analysis and one necessary orthogonal validation. A third method must replace a named analysis or contribute decision information the approved pair cannot provide.

## Result Interpretation And Project Progress

- Exploration, formalisation, and submission-preparation modes prevent final-submission overhead from being imposed on early scientific exploration.
- A read-only established-project importer inventories figures, plot data, analysis scripts, renderers, and captions; identifies conflicting versions; and requires explicit researcher confirmation of proposed relations.
- Versioned domain-context profiles keep literature-supported knowledge separate from current-project observations and bring organism, tissue, developmental stage, disease state, and cellular compartment into scientific review.
- Interpretation self-correction starts from observations, effect magnitude, uncertainty, experimental unit, negative findings, and competing explanations. A major problem leads to revised interpretation, a narrower claim, reanalysis, or method replacement rather than another checklist.

## Cell Communication And Secreted Signals

- CellChat uses official R workflows to create one object per biological sample, accepts Matrix Market, Seurat v5, and SingleCellExperiment inputs, retains sample-level results, and exports official circle, chord, and bubble views.
- CellChat 2.2.0 completed an observed two-sample controlled execution and output reload. Its current maturity is `EXECUTED_FIXTURE`; this release does not call it public biological-case validation.
- SecAct is parameterised through the official `SecAct.activity.inference.scRNAseq` and `SecAct.CCC.scRNAseq` APIs, with relative secreted-protein activity kept distinct from ligand–receptor scores. SecAct was not installed in the acceptance environment and remains `CONTRACT_ONLY`.
- The existing LIANA–CellPhoneDB public case continues to validate that exact method only; it does not transfer status to CellChat, NicheNet, direct CellPhoneDB, or SecAct.

## Scientific Figures And Research Story

- Every manuscript panel receives one distinct role in discovery, source or context, mechanistic consistency, orthogonal validation, boundary or negative evidence, or integration. Panels that repeat the same contribution do not automatically enter the main figure.
- Visual-semantic comparison between reference and redrawn panels examines aspect ratio, occupied content, regional information density, palette, and vector labels. Final review still checks plot family, UMAP or spatial geometry, colour meaning, clipping, occlusion, and legend proportion.
- New bilingual [Scientific Interpretation, Research Story, And Result Decisions](../capabilities/scientific-interpretation-and-storytelling.md) documentation is connected to the READMEs, capability map, usage guide, project organisation, architecture, and figure guidance.

## Maturity Display

Public views use only `CONTRACT_ONLY`, `EXECUTED_FIXTURE`, `PUBLIC_CASE_VALIDATED`, and `CURRENT_PROJECT_VALIDATED`. A multi-method capability inherits a shared level only when all declared methods meet it; otherwise the executed and public-case-validated methods are listed separately.

## Validation

The local complete suite ran 1,361 tests: 1,359 passed and 2 were skipped under declared conditions. A further 15 pytest-style single-cell and spatial scientific contracts passed with one non-blocking class warning. Release validation, the 6/6 strict health check, and plugin-contract validation also passed. Formal publication additionally requires all remote continuous-integration jobs for the same commit to succeed; the release commit and GitHub Actions results are the authoritative remote record.
