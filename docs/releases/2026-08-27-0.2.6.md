# 0.2.6 · 2026-08-27

[English](2026-08-27-0.2.6.md) · [中文](2026-08-27-0.2.6.zh-CN.md) · [Release index](README.md)

This release gives the project a clearer bilingual public introduction and closes the language-revision loop for manuscripts and research proposals. The scientific methods and their evidence levels remain governed by the existing module-specific contracts.

## Bilingual project introduction

- The Chinese and English README now begin from the user's research question and explain the path from study design and multi-scale data to analysis, interpretation and research delivery.
- Capability groups distinguish data scales and research objects from project-wide literature, database, integration, figure and writing support.
- Separate Chinese and English overview illustrations use the same restrained visual grammar and exact language-specific labels. Their final stage is research delivery rather than experimental execution.
- Installation remains a short natural-language request for Codex, with detailed usage, capability, evidence-map and release documents available through the bilingual document index.

## Academic voice revision for manuscripts and proposals

- `academic-prose-revision-audit` now exposes a document-specific revision contract. Manuscripts use restrained, results-forward prose; funding proposals retain a clear scientific vision when authentic preliminary evidence and a credible plan support it.
- Every manuscript or proposal revision follows three required stages: pre-revision review, scientific-content-preserving revision, and exact post-revision comparison.
- Numbers, results, equations, citations, technical terms, mechanism boundaries and evidence-calibrated uncertainty are protected. An author sample informs rhythm, connective habits, hedging and terminology without supplying project-specific wording.
- A revision that changes protected content, strengthens a claim beyond its evidence, or fails the post-revision pass cannot be delivered as final prose.

## Proposal abstracts and life-science language

- The Chinese abstract is written after the body is stable and remains within 400 characters including punctuation under the installed 2026 profile.
- The English abstract is derived from the Chinese scientific argument without a separate workbench length limit. It preserves the same biological entities, mechanism boundary, aims, uncertainty and expected knowledge advance rather than translating word for word or adding claims.
- The complete proposal is revised from title through captions and references so internal engineering, audit and software-orchestration language is replaced by natural life-science argument while genuine computational methods, parameters and quantitative facts remain precise.

## Validation

- Both changed writing modules passed their controlled compatibility cases and output reload checks.
- The release validator passed with 215 registered modules and 216 compatibility rows.
- The strict installed-package health contract passed 6 of 6 checks.
- The full regression suite ran 1,327 tests successfully, with 2 declared skips. These checks establish engineering consistency for this release; they do not promote an arbitrary manuscript, proposal or scientific project to publication-ready status.
