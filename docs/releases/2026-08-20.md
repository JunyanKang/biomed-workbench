# 0.2.1 · 2026-08-20

[English](2026-08-20.md) · [中文](2026-08-20.zh-CN.md) · [Release index](README.md)

This release extends the publication and scholarly-delivery layer without changing the 0.2 scientific kernel contract. It brings the reviewed Academic Humanizer and Nature-series research practices into the workbench's own module, routing, evidence, and test architecture rather than loading those external skills at runtime.

## Academic reading and writing

- Added evidence-preserving academic prose review for manuscripts and grant proposals, including explicit checks of numbers, results, equations, citations, terminology, structure, and claim strength before and after revision.
- Added full-paper bilingual reader-package review with stable page and text-block locations, terminology records, translation notes, and figure or table traceability.
- Added multi-source literature-landscape and authorised-acquisition record review. Citation count and journal identity remain reading-priority signals rather than evidence of claim support or study quality.
- Added proposal-foundation review covering the research canon, claim–evidence relationships, section boundaries, aims, feasibility evidence, independent value, and fallback plans.

## Reporting and research delivery

- Added statistical-reporting review centred on the true experimental unit, replication structure, assumptions, multiplicity, effect estimates, uncertainty, and consistency between text and figures.
- Added data-availability review that maps each result-supporting item to an explicit access route without inventing repositories, identifiers, licences, approvals, or restrictions.
- Added traceable experiment-record standardisation and presentation-package reload checks, while retaining raw-material identity, source assets, unresolved ambiguity, and rendered visual review as separate requirements.
- Tightened routing so generic table or matrix language does not incorrectly select peer-review response workflows.

## Integration and provenance

- The single `biomed-workbench` research entry now describes the new scholarly workflows alongside the existing citation, journal, reviewer-response, figure, patent, and project-evidence modules.
- The public capability guides, registry index, compatibility records, execution-readiness summaries, taxonomy, and installation evidence were regenerated from the current source.
- Third-party acknowledgements record the reviewed upstream projects and licence terms. External skill packages, account state, credentials, and host-specific configuration are not bundled.

## Verification scope

The nine new modules are deterministic, parameterised audit implementations exercised with controlled normal, boundary, and adverse fixtures. This establishes executable contract-level capability; it does not constitute validation of a particular manuscript, grant, experiment, literature corpus, or presentation. Existing biological public-data evidence was not reissued because these additions do not alter the underlying omics or molecular-analysis implementations.
