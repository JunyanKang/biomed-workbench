# Xenograft Tumor Growth

The xenograft-tumor-growth module preserves every animal's longitudinal tumor-volume observations, summarizes group trajectories, and calculates endpoint TGI only when the declared control group has positive mean growth and each included animal has baseline and endpoint observations. TGI is descriptive, not a survival, toxicity, statistical-significance, causal-efficacy, or biological-mechanism conclusion.

Study records must retain randomization, blinding, exclusions, volume method, endpoint and euthanasia policies, treatment exposure, and adverse events. The generated Python template preserves request and result provenance.
