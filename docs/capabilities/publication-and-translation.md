# Publication And Translation

## Scientific Role

This capability area turns the research record into a coherent, reviewable, and traceable scientific package. It links claims to evidence and analysis artifacts, preserves negative and unresolved findings, and treats revision as a verifiable transformation rather than a prose promise.

## Scientific Figures And Manuscripts

- Specify figures from scientific claims, source artifacts, uncertainty, and panel-level validation needs.
- Audit manuscript readiness, citation metadata, assertion coverage, temporal consistency, and claim-evidence integrity.
- Build a content-addressed manuscript revision base and apply review-driven patches with parent-child lineage.
- Keep untouched manuscript blocks unchanged and verify claimed analysis, experiment, figure, citation, and response locations.

Representative modules include `figure-specification`, `manuscript-audit`, `citation-audit`, `assertion-citation-coverage-audit`, `claim-evidence-integrity-audit`, `manuscript-revision-base`, and `manuscript-revision-lineage`.

## Peer Review And Revision

- Produce claim-linked reviewer assessments that distinguish major validity concerns from presentation issues.
- Build reviewer response matrices with evidence, action, manuscript location, and unresolved conflict fields.
- Audit consistency among declared research contracts, artifacts, methods, figures, and claims.
- Require an editor-priority decision when reviewer requests conflict materially.

Representative modules include `reviewer-assessment`, `response-matrix`, and `research-contract-consistency-audit`.

## Translation And Intellectual Property

- Audit research invention disclosures for evidence, novelty framing, enabling support, contributors, and unresolved ownership or disclosure questions.
- Connect molecular, omics, clinical, and experimental evidence to translational hypotheses without collapsing prediction into validation.
- Prepare a research package suitable for manuscript, review, response, patent, or presentation workflows while preserving audience-specific boundaries.

The principal dedicated module is `patent-disclosure-audit`, combined with the evidence and analysis modules relevant to the project.

## Quality Gates

A citation marker is not proof of claim support. A completed analysis is not automatically fit for publication. Manuscript revisions are blocked when evidence is missing, structural changes are unacknowledged, reviewer comments are incompletely extracted, locations do not resolve, placeholders remain, or the revision record cannot be verified. Patent and translational outputs retain uncertainty and do not substitute for legal or regulatory review.

## Typical Deliverables

Figure plans, manuscripts, methods, evidence tables, reviewer reports, response letters, response matrices, revision lineages, citation and claim audits, patent disclosures, presentation plans, and final research packages with visible limitations.
