# Publication And Translation

Languages: [English](publication-and-translation.md) · [中文](publication-and-translation.zh-CN.md)

## Scientific Role

This capability area turns the research record into coherent, reviewable, and traceable scientific material. It connects conclusions, evidence, and analysis results, preserves negative and unresolved findings, and ensures that manuscript changes correspond to the data, figures, and citations.

## Unified Publication Program

For manuscript, response, patent, or presentation requests, the workbench first assesses journal fit and article type, then establishes the figure and manuscript structure. It reviews citations, statements, temporal relationships, and support for conclusions, and uses response matrices and revision records to organise required changes. Patent and presentation material uses only reviewed evidence while retaining unresolved questions.

Manuscript revision, reviewer response, patent preparation, and presentation material are reviewed separately because they have different audiences, purposes, and quality requirements.

## Journal Positioning And Manuscript Requirements

Journal targeting uses individually maintained standards that cover:

- audience and scientific remit;
- preferred article families and project-fit terms;
- expected manuscript sections and language style;
- stated length, abstract, reference, display-item and related numeric constraints when an exact value applies to the selected article type;
- figure, reporting and policy requirements;
- the author instructions used for the assessment;
- the standards version and review date used for the assessment.

Journal ranking considers scientific scope, audience, article type, and evidentiary fit. The manuscript is checked item by item against the selected journal. When a numerical requirement is unspecified or depends on the article type, it remains a manual check and cannot support a submission-ready conclusion.

The standards version is preserved with the recommendation and review so that later revisions can be compared against the same requirements. See [Journal Positioning And Manuscript Standards](../journal-standards.md).

## Scientific Figures And Manuscripts

- Specify figures from scientific conclusions, source data, uncertainty, and the validation needs of each figure part.
- Audit manuscript readiness, citation metadata, assertion coverage, temporal consistency, and claim-evidence integrity.
- Establish a manuscript revision baseline and record each review-driven change.
- Keep untouched manuscript blocks unchanged and verify claimed analysis, experiment, figure, citation, and response locations.

When the target journal is unknown or its author-guidance version has not been confirmed, journal targeting is completed before manuscript review.

## Peer Review And Revision

- Produce claim-linked reviewer assessments that distinguish major validity concerns from presentation issues.
- Build reviewer response matrices with evidence, action, manuscript location, and unresolved conflict fields.
- Review consistency among study design, analysis results, methods, figures, and conclusions.
- Require an editor-priority decision when reviewer requests conflict materially.

## Translation And Intellectual Property

- Audit research invention disclosures for evidence, novelty framing, enabling support, contributors, and unresolved ownership or disclosure questions.
- Trace each proposed claim feature to declared source blocks and keep `explicit`, `inherent`, `needs-confirmation`, and `unsupported` support states separate.
- Prevent formal-claim drafting from silently carrying features that remain unsupported or require confirmation; retain those features as explicit follow-up work instead.
- Check bounded Chinese claim drafts for consecutive numbering, forward or missing references, confirmation placeholders, and result-oriented wording before formal review.
- Validate structured patent drafts across their source map, evidence ledger, claim-feature map, terminology, formulas, figures, specification, abstract, and declared delivery thresholds.
- Create a portable black-and-white SVG method flowchart for technical-draft review.
- Connect molecular, omics, clinical, and experimental evidence to translational hypotheses without collapsing prediction into validation.
- Prepare a research package suitable for manuscript, review, response, patent, or presentation workflows while preserving audience-specific boundaries.

These functions prepare scientific and technical material rather than legal conclusions. Formal patent language still requires review by a qualified professional.

## Checks Before Delivery

A citation marker is not proof of claim support. A completed analysis is not automatically fit for publication. Manuscript revisions are blocked when evidence is missing, structural changes are unacknowledged, reviewer comments are incompletely extracted, locations do not resolve, placeholders remain, or the revision record cannot be verified. Patent and translational outputs retain uncertainty and do not substitute for legal or regulatory review.

## Typical Deliverables

Figure plans, manuscripts, methods, evidence tables, reviewer reports, response letters, response matrices, revision lineages, citation and claim audits, patent disclosures, presentation delivery plans, and final research packages with visible limitations.

Presentation material is organised around its audience, maps key findings to their evidence, plans slides by content, and checks unresolved reviewer comments, evidence gaps, and reproducibility concerns before delivery.
